Small-set chasing lower bounds

Prove the stated randomized competitive lower bound for small-set chasing in finite metric spaces.

Task results ↓

Mathematical setting

The research question

The theorem comes from the theory of online algorithms. In small-set chasing on a finite metric space, an online algorithm controls one server; each request is a finite subset S of points, and serving it means moving into S, paying the distance travelled. The theorem states:

There are a universal constant c > 0 and a threshold n₀ such that for every n ≥ n₀ there is an n-point metric space M and a start point for which small-set chasing admits a distributional lower bound of c·(log₂ n / log₂ log₂ n)²: a finite sample space carrying a distribution of request sequences with positive expected optimal cost such that every deterministic online algorithm pays, in expectation, at least c·(log₂ n / log₂ log₂ n)² times the expected optimal cost.
