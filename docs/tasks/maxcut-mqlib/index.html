Maximum cut

Split a weighted graph into two parts to maximize the weight of crossing edges.

Task results ↓

The research question

In the maximum cut problem you are given an undirected graph with a (possibly negative, possibly fractional) weight on every edge, and you must split the vertices into two sides so that the total weight of the edges crossing the split — endpoints on different sides — is as large as possible. MaxCut is one of Karp's 21 NP-complete problems. For nonnegative edge weights, the Goemans–Williamson algorithm gives an approximately 0.87856-approximation. This benchmark also permits negative weights, beyond the assumptions of that guarantee.
