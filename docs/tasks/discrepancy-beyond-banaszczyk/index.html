Discrepancy beyond Banaszczyk

Prove a square-root discrepancy bound for column-sparse matrices in the stated large-sparsity regime.

The research question

The theorem comes from discrepancy theory. Given a matrix A ∈ {0,±1}^{m×n} where every column has at most k non-zero entries, the discrepancy is the minimum over ±1 colorings x of ‖Ax‖∞ — how evenly the columns can be split into two parts. It is conjectured that disc(A) = O(√k) for every k. The theorem (sparse_column_discrepancy_large_k) states: there are constants c, C > 0 such that every {0,±1} matrix A with at most k non-zero entries per column and k ≥ c (log n)² has a ±1 coloring with ‖Ax‖∞ ≤ C √k (for 2 ≤ n).
