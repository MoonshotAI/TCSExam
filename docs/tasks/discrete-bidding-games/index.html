Discrete bidding games

Prove that a player meeting the budget threshold has a legal strategy that keeps energy nonnegative.

Task results ↓

Mathematical setting
B≥Th⁡(v) ⟹ a winning strategyB\ge\operatorname{Th}(v)\ \Longrightarrow\ \text{a winning strategy}B≥Th(v) ⟹ a winning strategy

The research question

The theorem comes from the theory of discrete-bidding games. An energy discrete-bidding game is played on a finite directed graph with integer edge weights and total budget k: each turn both players bid, the higher bidder moves the token and pays the opponent; Pres wins iff the energy stays non-negative forever. The theorem states: there exists a legal, budget-agnostic Pres strategy that M-wins from every configuration ⟨v, B⟩ with B ≥ Th(v), where Th(v) is the threshold budget (the minimal budget with finite energy threshold) and M = (k + 1)·(W + max_v Energy(v, Th(v))) is explicit. The determinacy of reachability discrete-bidding games is given as the hypothesis hdet.
