Capacitated vehicle routing

Route vehicles from a depot to all customers, respecting capacity and the instance’s fleet limit while minimizing total travel length.

The research question

In the capacitated vehicle routing problem (CVRP), a depot serves customers with integer demands using identical vehicles of capacity Q. Design routes that start and end at the depot and together visit every customer exactly once. The total demand on every route must be at most Q.

Where the instance specifies a fleet limit K, the solution may use at most K routes. The task checker enforces the limit encoded by the -kK suffix of the instance name. Among valid route collections, minimize the total travel length.
