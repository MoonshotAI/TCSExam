Ryser’s conjecture

Prove or refute the proposed relation between matching and covering in partite uniform hypergraphs.

Task results ↓

Mathematical setting
τ(H)≤(r−1)ν(H)\tau(H)\le(r-1)\nu(H)τ(H)≤(r−1)ν(H)

The research question

An r-partite r-uniform hypergraph divides vertices into r parts, with each hyperedge containing one vertex from each part. A matching consists of pairwise disjoint hyperedges.

A vertex cover meets every hyperedge. Ryser’s conjecture bounds the size of a smallest such cover by r−1 times the size of a largest matching.

Current status: OpenAI has published counterexamples and Lean proofs in its public mathematics release. The benchmark retains the statement fixed for evaluation. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 and Kimi K3.1 Pro retrieved and adapted that public proof to the task’s fixed definitions in the evaluated runs.
