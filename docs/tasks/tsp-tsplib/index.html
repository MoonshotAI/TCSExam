Traveling salesperson

Find a short tour that visits every city exactly once and returns to its start.

The research question

The traveling salesman problem — given n cities and pairwise distances, find the shortest round trip visiting every city exactly once — is the emblematic NP-hard problem of combinatorial optimization.
