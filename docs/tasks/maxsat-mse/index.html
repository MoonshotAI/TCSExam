Weighted MaxSAT

Satisfy all hard clauses and minimize the weight of unsatisfied soft clauses.

The research question

In weighted MaxSAT you are given a Boolean formula in conjunctive normal form — an AND of OR-clauses over variables x1..xn — split into hard clauses, which must all be satisfied, and soft clauses, each carrying a positive integer weight. The cost of an assignment is the sum of the weights of the soft clauses it falsifies; the task is to find a hard-satisfying assignment of minimum cost. MaxSAT is NP-hard, and it is the workhorse encoding for a long list of applied optimization problems (planning, scheduling, verification, data analysis).
