Nurse rostering

Assign shifts to nurses to cover daily demand exactly and minimize contract and preference penalties.

Task results ↓

The research question

The nurse rostering problem: you are given a planning period for one hospital ward, a set of shift types (e.g. Early/Late/Day/Night), a team of nurses each working under a contract (work regulations), a daily demand (how many nurses are needed per shift type per day), and the nurses' day-off and shift-off requests. Assign at most one shift per nurse per day so that the demand is covered exactly, and minimize the total penalty for broken soft constraints (contract violations such as too many working days in a row, incomplete weekends, unwanted shift successions, and unmet requests).
