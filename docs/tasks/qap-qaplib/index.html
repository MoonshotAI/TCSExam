Quadratic assignment

Assign facilities to distinct locations to minimize the sum of flow multiplied by distance.

The research question

The quadratic assignment problem (QAP): you are given n facilities and n locations, a flow matrix F (flow between facilities) and a distance matrix D (distance between locations). Assign each facility to a distinct location — a permutation p where p[i] is the location of facility i — minimizing the total flow-by-distance cost

cost(p) = sum_{i,j} F[i][j] * D[p[i]][p[j]]

QAP is NP-hard (it contains TSP as a special case) and, unless P = NP, no constant-factor approximation exists (Sahni–Gonzalez 1976).
