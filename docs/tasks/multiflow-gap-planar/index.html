Planar multiflow and multicut

Prove the cactus-graph decomposition bound underlying the stated planar multiflow–multicut result.

The research question

The theorem is a graph statement underlying a lower bound on the multiflow–multicut ratio. A small-diameter decomposition distribution SDD(G, 2w, p) randomly chooses edges to remove. Any two vertices left in the same connected component must have distance < 2w in the original graph G, before those edges were removed. Each edge is removed with probability at most p.

A cactus is a graph in which every edge belongs to at most one cycle. The theorem states: for even w ≥ 2 and 0 < p < 1, if every finite cactus graph admits an SDD(G, 2w, p) distribution, then 8/7 ≤ w·p.

Through the equivalence between these decompositions and multiflow–multicut ratios, this yields a lower bound of 16/7 for cactus graphs and hence for planar graphs. That equivalence is background context; the formal task proves the graph-decomposition statement.
