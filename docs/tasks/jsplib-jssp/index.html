Job-shop scheduling

Schedule ordered operations on shared machines to minimize the final completion time.

The research question

In the job-shop scheduling problem (JSSP) you are given n jobs and m machines. Each job is a fixed chain of m operations; every operation occupies one specific machine for a given processing time. A machine runs at most one operation at a time; a job's operations must run in their given order. The goal is to minimize the makespan — the moment the last operation finishes.

JSSP is one of the classical NP-hard problems of scheduling theory.
