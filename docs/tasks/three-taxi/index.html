The online three-taxi problem

Prove a constant competitive ratio for the specified three-taxi algorithm on general metric spaces.

Task results ↓

Mathematical setting

The research question

The theorem comes from online algorithms. In the (hard) k-taxi problem, k taxis move in a metric space and must serve online requests (r, s): some taxi must move to the pick-up point r (this empty movement is the only cost) and is then relocated to s for free. The theorem states: there is a constant κ > 0 such that the algorithm TripodTracker for k = 3, as given by the trajectory semantics of the definition layer, has total online cost at most κ times the total offline cost (no additive constant), for every initial state with coinciding online/offline configurations and zero intervals and every request sequence paired with offline choices.
