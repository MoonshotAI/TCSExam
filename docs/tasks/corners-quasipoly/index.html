Quasipolynomial corners bounds

Bound the size of a subset of a finite abelian group squared that contains no nontrivial corner.

Task results ↓

Mathematical setting
(x,y), (x+d,y), (x,y+d),d≠0(x,y),\ (x+d,y),\ (x,y+d),\quad d\ne0(x,y), (x+d,y), (x,y+d),d=0

The research question

The theorem comes from additive combinatorics. A corner in G × G (for a finite abelian group G) is a triple (x, y), (x + d, y), (x, y + d) with d ≠ 0. The theorem states: there is a constant c > 0 such that for every finite abelian group G, every corner-free A ⊆ G × G satisfies |A| ≤ |G|²·exp(−c·(log|G|)^{1/600}).
