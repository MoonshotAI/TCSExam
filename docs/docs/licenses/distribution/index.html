Resource access and distribution conditions ​

This guide lists access routes and sharing conditions for TCSExam resources. It is for users preparing data locally or distributing source, data or images.

Apply conditions to the selected material ​

Use tcsexam build to acquire fixed official inputs, verify and transform them, and assemble local images. Resources marked local_prepare can be prepared locally under their source terms. private_only excludes a material from public source export and public image distribution.

Before redistributing a product, follow its resource-specific conditions below and retain the required source, attribution and notices. Each row applies to the named material and version; an image combines the conditions of all its inputs and inherited layers. Locally generated products retain applicable restrictions on their source materials. Use the linked records for exact product IDs and file scopes.
