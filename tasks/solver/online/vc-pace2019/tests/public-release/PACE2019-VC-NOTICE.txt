Modifications made for the fixed public development data product:
  The 100 odd-numbered public/vc-exact_NNN.gr files, NNN=001,003,...,199,
  were compressed with XZ Utils 5.2.5 using:
    --format=xz --stdout --check=crc64 -9
  The outputs were renamed to app/instances/vc-exact_NNN.xz and their modes
  were normalized to 0644. The graph plaintext was not changed. The output
  manifest records every path, byte count, SHA-256 and mode.
