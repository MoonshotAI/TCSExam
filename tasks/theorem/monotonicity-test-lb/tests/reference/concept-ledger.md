## 3. Statement-level discrepancies (itemized, with reasons)

1. **Parameterize with n = m²:** At the start of §3, the paper says “we always assume that √n is an integer” (this sentence was confirmed by independent retranslation; pdftotext displaced the square-root sign). The task uses m as the base parameter, with n := m² and s := m, N := 2^m, and quantifies over m. This is the faithful reading of the paper's convention; the lower bound along square values gives the asymptotic bound for all n by standard padding.
2. **Make all asymptotic notation explicit:** Every o(1) / Ω(1) is rewritten using existential constants and an m₀ threshold. c3's two Ω(1) statements become `∃ ε c > 0` (in the paper the implicit constants shrink exponentially with ℓ); c4's `1 + o(1)` becomes `1 + 2|B|/n` with assumption `2|B| ≤ n` (the exact meaning of o(1) is `|∪D_u|/n`, independently confirmed by retranslation and proof; `1/(1−t) ≤ 1+2t` for `0 ≤ t ≤ 1/2`); c5's o(n) conclusion becomes the explicit bound `4^{2ℓ−1}·(2q)^{1+1/(2ℓ)}·K` (this is the bound established in the paper's proof; o(n) follows after substituting q); c7's `1 − o(1)` becomes `∀ δ > 0 ∃ m₀`.
3. **Base of logarithms is 2:** The paper does not state the base. Claim 28's conclusion `50^t > ω(n⁵)` with t = log n holds only for base 2 (`50^{log₂n} = n^{log₂50} ≈ n^{5.64}`; the natural logarithm gives only n^{3.91}). Independent retranslation reaches the same conclusion. Counting statements (c6 and the good-outcome condition `100·s·log₂n`) use `Nat.log 2`; the query bound uses `Real.logb 2`.
4. **The main theorem uses the distributional-tester form:** Theorem 17 literally says that every two-sided-error adaptive tester must make Ω̃(n^{0.5−c}) queries. The shipped statement is the endpoint of its proof (§4, second paragraph: “We prove that no q-query, deterministic algorithm ALG can distinguish Dyes from Dno under the stronger oracle,” together with the paper's averaging argument): there exist ε, δ > 0 and m₀ such that for all m ≥ m₀, no randomized algorithm whose deterministic members all have depth at most q can simultaneously satisfy “yes acceptance rate ≥ 1−δ” and “joint probability of no and ε-far with acceptance ≤ δ.” Equivalence to the literal statement: c1 (yes support is entirely monotone) + c3 (no is ε-far with probability at least c) show that a tester with error ≤ δ must satisfy these two properties, so the shipped statement implies the literal one; the converse follows from the paper's amplification argument. Since this holds for the strong oracle, it also holds for the standard membership-query oracle (final sentence of §3.4; quoted verbatim in the shipped docstring).
5. **Repair the `|R|` assumption in Claim 28:** The paper prints `|R| ≤ n/2`, but the binomial coefficient `binom(√n−|R|, t)` in its proof is not meaningful under this assumption, and the final inequality chain uses `√n−|R| ≥ √n/2`. At the application site, `|R| = |R_e| ≤ |Q| ≤ q ≤ s/2` for large m. The shipped assumption is repaired to `2|R| ≤ s` (independent retranslation reaches the same conclusion; precedents for the same kind of repair are the ledgers for lean-gg26-proximity and lean-hdx-list-decoding).
6. **Lemma 8 is not a separate tested statement:** The proof of Lemma 9 is a four-line consequence of the two observations in Lemma 8, folded into c1; its content is in c1's guided-tier docstring. Likewise for Facts 11/12/16 (see the folded-in results note in Section 1).
7. **Queries outside the middle layer do not update the outcome:** The paper assumes the algorithm queries only middle-layer points (values of f outside the truncated region are known), “without loss of generality.” The shipped oracle returns the truncated category and the known function value outside the middle layer; `addPoint` leaves the outcome unchanged. This is equivalent under the paper's assumption (with the stronger-oracle model, this does not change the lower bound).
8. **Make boundary conditions explicit:** State ℓ ≥ 1 everywhere (the paper says it is a positive integer constant); c4 includes 2 ≤ n (the paper assumes n is sufficiently large); c6 includes `1 ≤ t` and `2t ≤ s` (t = ⌊log₂n⌋ ≥ 1 requires n ≥ 2; the degree lower bound requires t ≤ s − |R|); c5's `hnested`/`hchild` require 1 ≤ j (Fact 12 specifies nonroot nodes; the root level is covered separately by `hroot`).

## 4. Step-by-step check of the main-theorem assembly (the c8 chain, checked item by item)

Each line gives one step in the definition-layer derivation: c8 follows from c1, c3, c4, c5, and c7; c2 follows through c3, and c6 through c7.

Take witnesses ε, c from c3 (`Pr_no[ε-far] ≥ c`, m ≥ m₀₁), and set δ := c/4. Let m₀ be the maximum of the c3 threshold, the c7 threshold at δ′ := c/8, and an analysis threshold ensuring `2B̄ ≤ n` and `2B̄/n ≤ c/8` (existence is shown below).

1. Assume for contradiction that some randomized algorithm (ι, A, w) satisfies `yesAccMix ≥ 1−δ` and `noAccFarMix ≤ δ`.
2. For each deterministic member Aᵢ: `noAcc(Aᵢ) = noAccFar(Aᵢ) + Pr_no[accept ∧ not far] ≤ noAccFar(Aᵢ) + (1 − c)` (c3). Summing with the weights gives `noAccMix ≤ δ + 1 − c = 1 − 3c/4`.
3. Gap: `yesAccMix − noAccMix ≥ (1 − δ) − (1 − 3c/4) = c/2` (corresponds to equation (2) in the paper).
4. By weighted averaging (`Σ wᵢ = 1`, weights nonnegative), there is a member A′ with `yesAcc(A′) − noAcc(A′) ≥ c/2`. A′ has depth ≤ qbound.
5. Running A′ on `f ∼ Dyes` ends in a good outcome with probability ≥ 1 − δ′ (c7, m ≥ m₀₂(δ′)).
6. For each good outcome O (its Q comes from the history, and `|Q| ≤ depth ≤ qbound`), instantiate c5 with `P := O.P`. The assumptions hnested / hchild / hroot are Fact 12 (provable inline from `addPoint`/`outcomeOf`; checked in small example E11); hmid is membership in the middle layer (only middle-layer points are inserted); hgodd/hgeven are the first two good-outcome clauses. Then `|B_O| ≤ 4^{2ℓ−1}·(2q)^{1+1/(2ℓ)}·250·m·log₂(m²)`. Substituting qbound gives `|B_O|/n ≤ 4^{2ℓ−1}·2^{1+1/(2ℓ)}·250·(log₂ n)^{−1/(2ℓ)} =: r(m)` (E8 numerically confirms this closed form). Choose m₀₃ so that `r(m) ≤ min(1/2, c/8)`; r(m) → 0, so this threshold is finite.
7. **Bridge fact (provable inline):** On sample (M,H), the run ends in O iff `outcomeOf M H O.Q = O` (induction over the history; the outcome is updated by inserting one point at a time). Therefore `Pr_yes[ends in O] = (1/#M)Σ_M yesRatio(M, O.Q, O)`; likewise on the no side (the two distributions have the same M marginal).
8. Apply c4 to each M. If M and O are inconsistent then `yesRatio = 0 ≤` the right-hand side trivially; otherwise, O good implies ρ is constant (third good-outcome clause) and `2|B_O| ≤ n` (step 6), so `yesRatio ≤ (1 + 2|B_O|/n)·noRatio ≤ (1 + c/8)·noRatio`.
9. Sum: `yesAcc(A′) ≤ Σ_{O good, accept} Pr_yes[ends in O] + δ′ ≤ (1 + c/8)·noAcc(A′) + δ′`, hence the gap is at most `c/8 + δ′ = c/4 < c/2`.
10. Contradiction with step 4. ∎

**Rate check:** `r(m) → 0` in step 6 is `|B_ε| = o(n)` at the end of §4.1 in the paper (the power of n cancels exactly; the entire loss is `(log n)^{−1/(2ℓ)}`, confirmed by E8 and independent retranslation). c8's δ = c/4 matches the paper's c_ℓ/4 (the paper then uses standard amplification to raise the error from c_ℓ/4 to 1/3; the shipped existential statement therefore implies the literal Theorem 17).

## 5. Assumptions of each tested statement (bciks20 rule: check every assumption, quantifier, and boundary)

Each bullet presents one tested statement and compares the paper's assumptions item by item with the shipped assumptions.

- **c1 `yes_monotone`:** The paper has no extra assumptions (arbitrary M and arbitrary family of per-leaf constants H). The shipped statement also has none; after truncated natural subtraction, it still holds case by case for small parameters (for example, f(x)=1 and `|x| < n/2−s` is impossible for small parameters; E2 exhaustively checked n=4). Quantifiers: ∀ M, ∀ family c of constants, ∀ x y (CubeLE allows equality, as in paper footnote 1).
- **c2 `dist_ge_violation_pairs`:** Paper assumptions: S consists of violating pairs whose endpoints are pairwise disjoint. Shipped: `hviol` (`x ≼ x′ ∧ f x = 1 ∧ f x′ = 0`; x ≠ x′ follows automatically from the different function values, as in the paper) + `hdisj` (sharing any endpoint implies it is the same pair; an equivalent formulation of endpoint-disjointness). Conclusion `|S|/2ⁿ ≤ dist`: shipped uses a pointwise count form for each g (`|S| ≤ disagreeCount f g`), equivalent to the paper after taking the minimum over all monotone g and dividing by 2ⁿ. Exhaustively checked in E5.
- **c3 `no_far_from_mono_whp`:** Paper assumption: ℓ is a positive constant (the constant shrinks exponentially with ℓ). Shipped: for all ℓ ≥ 1, there exist ε, c, m₀. The paper's three conditions on edge set X (x_s = 0, x* = x^{s}, `(n/2)−√n ≤ |x| ≤ (n/2)+√n−1`; same ΓM leaf; anti-dictator) appear only in the proof, not the statement. It depends on Fact 4 (the middle-layer size is Θ(2ⁿ), a standard but unpublished expert-known result; a standard corollary of binomial concentration, retained as an inline obligation in the c3 proof; see Section 6).

- **c4 `safe_outcome_ratio`:** Paper assumptions: O is safe (ρ is constant and `|B| = o(n)`). Shipped: M and O are consistent (`Consistent` = first two clauses of Fact 11; the paper's proof explicitly says at the outset that M may be assumed consistent, otherwise both sides are zero) + ρ is constant + `2|B| ≤ n` (made explicit; see discrepancy 2). The conclusion `α_M ≤ (1+2|B|/n)·β_M` matches the per-tree content of the paper's `α ≤ (1+o(1))β` (`α_M = 2^{−m′}`, `β_M ≥ (1−|B|/n)·2^{−m′}`; boundary case m′ = 0: α_M = 1, β_M = 1, so 1 ≤ 1 holds). Numerically checked in E7.
- **c5 `good_outcome_danger_union_bound`:** Paper assumptions: O is good (all three clauses of Definition 18) and `|Q| ≤ q`. Shipped: an abstract family of P sets + structural assumptions (hnested = Fact 12(1), including the “nonroot” restriction; hchild = Fact 12(2); hroot = the root version of Fact 12; hmid = the middle-layer assumption from Fact 16) + the first two good-outcome conditions (hgodd/hgeven; the third condition on ρ is irrelevant to this lemma and is not used in the paper's proof). Conclusion: the paper states `|B_ε| = o(n)`, but its proof establishes `|B_ε| ≤ 4^{2ℓ−1}·Σ_a|P_a|^{1+1/(2ℓ)}·K ≤ 4^{2ℓ−1}·(2q)^{1+1/(2ℓ)}·K` (the last step uses `Σ p^{1+α} ≤ (Σ p)^{1+α}` and the root version of Fact 12). The shipped version states this explicit bound. The constant chain 100 (good outcome) → 150 (the min(|P_u|², |P_v|) form in Claims 21/22) → 250 (K) was checked term by term.
- **c6 `term_avoidance_ratio`:** Paper assumptions: A ⊆ [n], Δ ⊆ A, `|Δ| ≥ K′ = 100√n log n`, `|R| ≤ n/2` (printed). Shipped: n = s², t = ⌊log₂n⌋, 1 ≤ t, 2t ≤ s, `|Δ| ≥ 100·s·t`, `2|R| ≤ s` (repaired; see discrepancy 5). Conclusion: the paper says `Pr[E2*] ≤ o(n⁻⁵)·Pr[E1*]`; its proof establishes `|V|/|U| ≤ 50^{−t}`. Shipped states `50^t·|V| ≤ |U|` (`50^t = n^{log₂50} > n⁵`, so the shipped form implies the printed conclusion). E10 checks the double-counting argument.
- **c7 `yes_run_good_whp`:** Paper assumption: ALG is a deterministic q-query algorithm (q from equation (1)). Shipped: for every δ > 0 there is m₀; depth ≤ qbound. The third good-outcome clause holds automatically under Dyes (first paragraph of §4.2 in the paper; checked by E12), so only the first two clauses need to hold with high probability, as in the paper. The four cases of the one-step lemma (Lemma 27) and the union bound (Fact 12(3): `(2ℓ+1)·|Q|` nonempty P sets) are folded into its proof. c6 is instantiated there with A = A_{u,1} (allowed coordinates on the term side), `Δ = {i ∈ A_{u,1} : x_i = 0}` (`|Δ| ≥ K′ = 100·s·log₂n`, matching the c6 assumption), and R = R_e (`|R_e| ≤ |Q| ≤ q ≤ s/2`, which gives `2|R| ≤ s` for large m).
- **c8 `monotonicity_testing_lb`:** Paper assumption: ℓ is a positive constant. Shipped: for all ℓ ≥ 1 there exist ε, δ, m₀; randomized algorithms are finite weighted mixtures of deterministic algorithms (nonnegative weights summing to 1). See Section 4 for the step-by-step synthesis.

## 6. Systematic error-check record (itemized under SOP 6.5)

1. **Are existentially quantified outputs constrained?** The existential quantifiers in c3/c8 range only over constants ε, c, δ and threshold m₀; they do not produce a construction. The main theorem asserts nonexistence of an algorithm, with no degenerate value space. Checks: c3 holds for arbitrarily small ε and c (not vacuous); c8 holds for small ε, δ and becomes false for larger ones—the direction is correct.
2. **Degenerate parameters:** ℓ = 1 (minimum case, L = 2, tree has two levels: terms + clauses) leaves content in all tested statements; for small m (n = 1, 4), the m₀ thresholds in c3/c7/c8 make the statements vacuously true (the standard property of an ∃m₀ form, not a vacuity defect); c1 holds case by case for small parameters (E2); in c6, when `100·s·t > n`, the premise cannot hold, so the conditional statement is true (as expected for a universally quantified implication; its premise is satisfiable for large m).
3. **Are the main theorem's assumptions at least those required where each tested statement is used?** Section 4 checks this step by step. At the c4 use site, its three assumptions (consistency, ρ constant, `2|B| ≤ n`) follow respectively from the construction (consistency, E11-i), the third good-outcome clause, and the explicit bound in step 6. All c5 structural assumptions hold for a run outcome by Facts 12/16 (E11). c7's depth assumption is exactly c8's quantifier condition. c3 is called without assumptions. c8's own assumptions (weights sum to 1, nonnegative weights, depth bound) match what the paper's Yao averaging argument needs.
4. **Do substitution and evaluation points match?** The exponent `1/2 − 1/(4ℓ+2)` in qbound matches equation (1) exactly; K = `250·s·log₂n`, K′ = `100·s·log₂n` match Definition 18 and Lemma 27 (log base in discrepancy 3); the c5 factor `4^{2ℓ−1}` and exponent `1 + 1/(2ℓ)` match equation (3) at k = 1; c6's factor 50 = `(√n/2)·(100√n)/(n)` was checked by canceling the numerator and denominator (t cancels).
5. **Base cases:** The induction base of c5 (k = 2ℓ−1) is inside its proof; the statement needs no base-case assumption (structural assumptions cover all levels). c6 explicitly includes 1 ≤ t (i.e. n ≥ 2) and 2t ≤ s. Params carries `0 < L`, `0 < N`.
6. **Two-sided conditions and boundaries:** The middle layer is a closed two-sided interval (`inMiddle`); truncation uses strict values outside on both sides (`talagrandFn`), exactly as in §3.2. `|x| ≤ (n/2)+√n−1` inside the c3 proof and its use of Fact 4 do not enter any statement. c4's `2|B| ≤ n` is the integral form of `t ≤ 1/2` required by `1/(1−t) ≤ 1+2t`; there is no off-by-one issue.
7. **Weakening and vacuity:** c4's conclusion `1 + 2|B|/n ≥ 1/(1−|B|/n)` holds for `0 ≤ |B|/n ≤ 1/2`, so it does not weaken the usable form of the paper's conclusion. c6's `50^t ≤ n^{log₂50}` is stronger than the reciprocal form of the paper's required o(n⁻⁵) (the larger lower bound on |U|/|V| is stronger); direction is correct. c8's two-conjunct impossibility is an equivalent packaging of the stronger “small distinguishing gap” form; neither one-sided case is omitted. No assumptions are jointly impossible: all c5 assumptions hold for genuine outcomes (E11); c6's assumptions are satisfiable for large n (item 2).

## 7. Scope not covered

- Theorem 2 (tight lower bound for constant-round adaptivity, §5, Lemmas 31/32) and Theorem 3 (relative-error model, Appendix B) are not formalized; the same definition layer can be reused in later work.
- Appendix A's matching algorithm (which shows the construction parameters are tight) is not formalized (a lower-bound task needs only directional conclusions).
- Fact 4 (the middle-layer size is Θ(2ⁿ)) is not a separate tested statement: it is standard binomial concentration (follows from Chebyshev) and is an inline obligation in the c3 proof; recorded here for reference.
- The printed expression for the second factor in the proof of Lemma 10, `1 − ((n−|x*|)/n)^{√n}`, differs from the standard calculation `1 − (|x*|/n)^{√n}` (independent retranslation issue 8 identifies a typo). After substituting the middle-layer bound, both give the same lower bound, so this does not affect any shipped statement—c3 is existential-constant form and does not use this factor.
