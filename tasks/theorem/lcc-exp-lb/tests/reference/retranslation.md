# Independent Retranslation Comparison — Six Statements for the Kothari–Manohar LCC Exponential Lower Bound

This note compares two independently prepared “translations,” records how differences were adjudicated, and gives the conclusions. It is one of three fidelity records for the L2 tier.

- Materials: Version A (shipped, `environment/app/Task/Basic.lean`, compiled successfully) was translated by a task-authoring subagent after close reading of the arXiv 2311.00558v1 PDF. Version B was independently retranslated from text extracted with `pdftotext` by a different subagent in a separate session; its author did not know the shape of the shipped statements. Adjudication uses page images of arXiv v1 (key formula pages among printed pages 2, 4, and 24–41, inspected page by page).

## Sources and independence of the two versions

- **Version A (shipped):** Based on a full reading of the arXiv v1 PDF, with page-image checks. It followed the SOP to split the material into six checkpoints, built the definition layer independently, and recorded each packaging deviation in §3 of `concept-ledger.md`.
- **Version B (independent retranslation):** Based on `pdftotext` output from the same paper (with formulas garbled in some places and inferred by the retranslator). It restated six lemmas as near-formal statements, spelling out premises, quantifiers, exponents, and boundary cases, and proactively marked ambiguous notation. Its author had not seen Version A's checkpoint breakdown or identifiers.
- The paper itself is the only shared reference, so the two versions were prepared independently.

## Remaining disagreement and conclusion

1. **Square root in Lemma 7.1:** the only substantive disagreement; the page image adjudicates in favor of Version A (see the table above). No checkpoint statement needs to change.
2. The list of paper typos flagged by Version B (the h range in the proof of Observation 5.2, z in ℋ_{i,Q,z}, “normally decodable” in §5, “(2, δ)-LDC” in §7.6, and a repeated ℋ subscript in the proof of Observation 6.2) does not affect the statement layer; all are recorded for reference.
3. **Conclusion:** all six checkpoint statements agree with the independent retranslation on every decision point, with Version A prevailing on the square-root issue after page-image adjudication. The suffix reading of Definition 6.1 (4) and the index ruling for condition 3 of Definition 7.2 each have independent confirmation from both versions. No change to the shipped statements is needed.
