# Independent Retranslation Comparison: The Three-Player XOR Parallel-Repetition Chain

This record compares two translations of the BBKLM25 chain, covering Lemmas 2.1, 2.2, and 2.3; Theorem 2; Corollary 4.1; Lemma 6.2; Theorem 3; and Theorem 1.

- Adjudication is based on the original ECCC TR25-040 (see Sections 1 and 3 of `concept-ledger.md`).
- This is the second of three fidelity records (independent retranslation).

## Paper-text errata and ambiguities identified by B (D1–D20)

Items relevant to the statements have been incorporated into the shipped version or `concept-ledger`: D1 (theorems omit an n range; conventionally add n ≥ 1); D2 (absolute-value bars around analytic quantities were lost in the text layer, confirmed in the rendered image); D3 (three omissions in the proof of Lemma 2.3); D4/D15 (reading of 1(S) and counterexample to the Hamming-weight reading); D5 (two inconsistent symbols for T, resolved as ω^{−t} following the statement); D6 (Corollary 4.1 prints “c := (𝒢,μ)”); D7 (D is a closed ball); D8 (the two versions of Definition 6.1 differ); D9 (Theorem 3 omits superscripts n and p from the ranges of f,g,h and does not state p is prime); a one-sentence reduction that “N is a power of p”; D10/D11 (k missing from the exponent in Lemma 8.1, and the lemma has no standalone assumption statement); D16 (the §6 version omits ∀x,x′ from Lemma 6.2); D17 (bipartite reading of Definition 1.2); D18 (meaning of no-Z-embeddings); D19 (Theorem 1's constant is summarized as c(G), while the body says c(G,µ)); D20 (Theorem 3 omits “uniform” from the probability subscript). The rest are text-layer artifacts that do not affect the statements: D12 (“Φ denote complex numbers” should be ℂ), D13 (the summary and body disagree on the domain of t), and D14 (an extraction remnant “Let Pf”).
